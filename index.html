The Best Seat

Two lights, impossibly far apart.
Let's place them side by side.

Sound on. About 4 minutes.

353,000 km

Earth, the best seat in the house,
erases a distance of 1.2 billion kilometers.

Even light needs over an hour to cross that distance.
Yet our eyes join the two in an instant.

Even if only in appearance,
that we can feel them side by side
may itself be a kind of salvation.
